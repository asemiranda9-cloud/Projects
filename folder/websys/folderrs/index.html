Women with Baskets - Anita Magsaysay-Ho

04 Laboratory Exercise 2

Artwork Information

This artwork portrays women from rural communities engaged in their daily tasks. It highlights the cultural tradition of basket weaving and the connection to food and sustenance. The serene expressions of the figures reflect dignity and calmness in labor. The use of earthy tones emphasizes harmony with nature. Overall, the piece celebrates resilience, simplicity, and the quiet strength of rural life.
